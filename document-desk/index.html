What to expect

This reconstructs documents from their content rather than copying pixels, so layout won't be pixel-identical to the source — headings, paragraphs, and tables carry over; exact fonts, colors, and positioning don't. Table detection inside PDFs is pattern-based and works best on documents with clear column alignment. Images embedded in a source document aren't currently re-embedded into a Word output.

This is an extractive summary: it selects your document's most information-dense existing sentences by word-frequency scoring, and returns them unchanged and in original order. It does not paraphrase, does not verify facts, and has no understanding of meaning — treat it as a fast way to surface likely-important sentences, not a substitute for reading anything where precision matters.
